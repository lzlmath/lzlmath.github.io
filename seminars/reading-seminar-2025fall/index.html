Reading Seminar on the Jacquet-Rallis Trace Formula

Fall 2025

This seminar focuses on the Jacquet-Rallis trace formula and its applications to the Gan-Gross-Prasad conjecture for unitary groups. The primary references for the seminar are:

  • [Jac86] H. Jacquet, Sur un résultat de Waldspurger .
  • [JR11] H. Jacquet and S. Rallis, On the Gross-Prasad Conjecture for Unitary Groups .
  • [Yun11] Z. Yun, The Fundamental Lemma of Jacquet and Rallis .
  • [Zha14] W. Zhang, Fourier Transform and the Global Gan-Gross-Prasad Conjecture for Unitary Groups .
  • [BP21] R. Beuzart-Plessis, A New Proof of the Jacquet-Rallis Fundamental Lemma .
  • [BPLZZ21] R. Beuzart-Plessis, Y. Liu, W. Zhang and X. Zhu, Isolation of Cuspidal Spectrum, with Application to the Gan-Gross-Prasad Conjecture .
  • [BPCZ22] R. Beuzart-Plessis, P.-H. Chaudouard and M. Zydor, The Global Gan-Gross-Prasad Conjecture for Unitary Groups: the Endoscopic Case .
  • [Zyd22] M. Zydor, Periods of Automorphic Forms over Reductive Subgroups .

We meet every Friday from 2:00 to 4:00 pm at Vincent Hall 364.
